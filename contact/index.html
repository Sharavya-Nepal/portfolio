What can Sharavya build for our business?+

We develop business websites, mobile applications, custom software, dashboards, admin portals, APIs, and integrations. We also help with the cloud and server systems behind them.

Can you help with an existing cloud or server setup?+

Yes. Our services include cloud architecture consulting, server management, migration, deployment planning, monitoring, and security hardening. We'll start by understanding your current setup and support needs.

Where does AI automation actually help?+

Practical starting points include lead handling, document processing, reporting, and customer communication. We focus on how a workflow fits your business, then identify the steps that could benefit from automation.

How do we get started?+

Email hello@sharavya.com with what you're working on, your current challenges, and any timeline you have in mind. That gives us a useful place to begin the conversation.
