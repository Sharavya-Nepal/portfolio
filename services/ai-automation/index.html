THE CONVERSATION BEFORE THE BUILD

Understand the workflow before automating it.

We look at the inputs, decisions, systems, and people involved. Define the information used, the outputs people need to review, and the limits of the workflow before deciding what to automate.

Our approach ↗
  1. Which steps are repetitive or slow?
  2. What information and tools does the process use?
  3. Where does a person need to review or decide?

POSSIBLE STARTING POINTS

What could this
look like for you?

These examples show how a project might take shape. The scope starts with your business, your tools, and the people involved.

01Enquiries with a clearer next step+

For teams repeatedly copying lead information between communication and business tools.

Map how an enquiry arrives, what information it needs, and who should act. Explore integrations or automation that support routing and follow-up without losing the human context.

Enquiry → Required information → Routing → Human follow-up

02Documents with less repetition+

For workflows that involve repeated reading, extracting, or organising information.

Identify the document types, required fields, and exceptions. If AI assistance is useful, define how outputs will be checked and where uncertain results need a person’s attention.

Document → Extraction → Review → Connected system

03Reporting that fits the work+

For teams gathering the same information from several places to prepare a recurring report.

Identify the sources and how their information should fit together. Plan the format, review step, and destination before deciding which parts can be automated.

Sources → Agreed structure → Review → Useful report

What business processes can you help automate?+

Our offering includes lead handling, document processing, reporting, customer communication, and business process automation. The right starting point depends on your workflow.

Does every automation need AI?+

No. The process should guide the choice of approach. Some steps need a straightforward integration or rule; others may benefit from AI assistance.

How do we identify a useful first project?+

Choose a clear, repeatable workflow with known inputs and an outcome your team can evaluate. Describe what happens today and which parts cause the most friction.
