POSSIBLE STARTING POINTS

What could this
look like for you?

These examples show how a project might take shape. The scope starts with your business, your tools, and the people involved.

01A clearer business website+

For a business whose customers need to understand its offering and take the next step.

Organise the services or products, explain what makes the business relevant, and make enquiry paths easy to find. Content, mobile layouts, and search visibility belong in the same plan.

Business goals → Content & structure → Interface → Launch

02A focused internal workspace+

For a team working across spreadsheets, messages, and repeated administrative tasks.

Map the information people need and the actions they take. A dashboard or admin portal can bring these tasks together with access and integrations planned around the actual workflow.

User tasks → Information & access → Dashboard → Integrations

03A connected customer product+

For an idea that needs a web or mobile application and connections to existing systems.

Define the essential user journey first. Decide which features make the first release useful, which services it depends on, and what can wait for a later stage.

Core journey → First-release scope → Application → Feedback
