FROM THE FIRST QUESTION TO WHAT COMES NEXT

A project is more
than a launch date.

Each stage answers a different question. Together, they keep the product, the business, and the people using it moving in the same direction.

0104
01 / DISCOVER

Make the problem clear.

Walk through the current experience, hear where work gets stuck, and identify the people and systems involved. An existing product or a rough idea is enough to begin.

WORK TOWARDShared goals, user priorities, and a focused project scope.
02 / DESIGN

See how it fits together.

Explore the customer journey alongside the architecture behind it. Make decisions about information, interfaces, integrations, and operating needs before implementation gathers pace.

WORK TOWARDA product direction and a practical technical plan.
03 / BUILD

Turn decisions into something useful.

Bring the interface, software, integrations, and infrastructure together. Review the important user journeys and prepare the release around the agreed scope.

WORK TOWARDA working release with a clear path to deployment.
04 / OPERATE

Keep the whole picture in view.

Plan for the work after launch: deployment, monitoring, server responsibilities, and future improvements. Agree on what needs ongoing support and who owns each part.

WORK TOWARDClear operating responsibilities and next priorities.
